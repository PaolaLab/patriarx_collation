# PatriarX Collation
This repository contains the collations of the manuscripts of the Cyril’s Lexicon carried out by the Research Units of Messina (Prof. Giuseppe Ucciarcello, postdoctoral researcher Federica Scognamiglio) and Basilicata (Prof. Elena Esposito, postdoctoral researcher Carmelo Nicolò Benvenuto) — and in one case by Verona (Prof. Paolo Scattolin, postdoctoral researcher Paola Carmela La Barbera). Messina Research Unit carried out the collation of the manuscripts preserved in Italian libraries and in the Vatican City. The Basilicata Research Unit carried out the collation of the manuscripts preserved in foreign libraries.
